(02) Method

Discover, plan, deliver, hand over

  1. 01

    Discover

    We talk to the people who use the system and read what exists: contracts, tools, data and past incidents. No changes yet.

  2. 02

    Plan

    We write down the options, costs and risks in plain language and agree an order of work that fits your budget.

  3. 03

    Deliver

    Small increments, shipped and visible every week, so you can steer before money goes in the wrong place.

  4. 04

    Hand over

    Documentation, training and a short list of next steps. You keep everything; nothing depends on us staying.
