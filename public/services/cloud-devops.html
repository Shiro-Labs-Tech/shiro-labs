(05) Questions

Short answers

Which cloud do you work with?

Mostly AWS, and comfortable on GCP, Azure or plain VPS hosting. We recommend what fits your size, not what is fashionable.

Do you provide on-call?

Not by default. We set things up so your team can be on call, and can discuss a support plan separately.

Will a migration cause downtime?

We plan migrations with a rehearsal and a rollback and aim for no visible downtime, though some cutovers need a short window.
