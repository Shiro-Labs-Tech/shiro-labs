(03) How it runs

Four steps, written down as we go

  1. 01

    Observe

    We watch people use the current tool and note where they slow down.

  2. 02

    Design

    Low-fidelity screens first, tested with the people who will use them.

  3. 03

    Build

    Components and screens in small releases, behind flags where needed.

  4. 04

    Hand over

    A documented design system and a backlog your team can pick up.
