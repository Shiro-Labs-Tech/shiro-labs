Problems we are usually called for

(02) What we do

Pick what applies, skip what does not

01

Use-case discovery

We list candidate tasks and rank them by time saved, risk and how easy the output is to check.

02

Assistants with review

Drafting replies, summaries and reports, with a person approving anything that leaves the building.

03

Workflow and agent automation

Multi-step tasks such as triage, data entry and routing, with limits on what the agent may do.

04

Evaluation sets

Real examples with the right answers, so every prompt, model or change is scored.

05

Security and data policy

What data may go to which model, where it is stored and how misuse is caught.

06

Monitoring and cost

Quality and spend tracked after launch, with alerts when either drifts.

(03) How it runs

Four steps, written down as we go

  1. 01

    Choose

    One task with clear value and a way to check the output.

  2. 02

    Pilot

    A narrow version with a person approving, running on real work.

  3. 03

    Measure

    Accuracy, time saved and cost per task, reported plainly, including the failures.

  4. 04

    Decide

    Expand, adjust or stop, based on the numbers.

(05) Questions

Short answers

Which models do you use?

We stay model-agnostic and choose on accuracy, cost and your data policy.

Is our data safe?

We agree a data policy before the pilot and use providers and settings that fit it. Sensitive data can stay in your own environment.

Do you build chatbots?

Sometimes, but we usually start with drafts and internal tools, where mistakes are cheap and a person checks the result.
