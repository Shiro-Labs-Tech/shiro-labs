(01) Where this helps

Problems we are usually called for

  1. 01

    Builds are slow enough that people batch changes

    Big batches hide bugs and make each release riskier. Fast feedback keeps changes small.

  2. 02

    Releases are a ritual

    A checklist, a Friday freeze and one person who knows the steps. We turn the ritual into a pipeline.

  3. 03

    Nobody trusts a green build

    When checks are flaky or missing, green means little. We make the signal worth trusting.

  4. 04

    Rollback means a hotfix at night

    A release you cannot undo quickly makes every deploy feel dangerous.

(03) How it runs

Four steps, written down as we go

  1. 01

    Observe

    We time every stage and map who waits on what.

  2. 02

    Plan

    A short list of changes ranked by minutes saved and risk removed.

  3. 03

    Build

    Changes ship one at a time, each measured against the baseline.

  4. 04

    Hand over

    Runbooks and a dashboard of pipeline health that your team can keep reading.
